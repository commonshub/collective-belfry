## What happens to your pass

A refunded pass ends straight away: you can no longer sign in to the apps, and the pass in your
wallet and on your phone stops working. Your village tokens stay in your wallet but are only
useful while you take part.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute the payment with your card provider instead of asking us, the pass ends as soon as
the dispute is opened. If the dispute is decided in our favour, the pass is valid again.

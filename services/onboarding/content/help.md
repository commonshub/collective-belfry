# Help

Everything you need for Open Source Village on one page.

## Signing in for the first time

1. After your pass is paid you get an e-mail, "Your Open Source Village pass is active". Open its **sign-in
   link** on the phone or computer you want to use. It works once, within 7 days.
2. That signs you in. You land on [your account]({{account}}): your pass, your village name and
   the apps.
3. Next time, open any app and choose **Sign in with Open Source Village**. Enter your e-mail
   address. We send you a link and a 6-digit code, both valid for 10 minutes: open the link in
   the same browser, or type the code on the sign-in page (handy on a device where your e-mail
   is not). Once you are signed in, the apps sign you in by themselves.

No e-mail? Check your spam folder, then ask a steward to send a new link.

## Your pass at the door

You have two ways:

- **On your phone, no app needed**: open [your pass]({{pass_page}}) and show the QR code. It
  renews itself every 30 seconds, so keep the page open; each code works for 90 seconds only.
- **In an EUDI wallet**: open the claim link in your activation e-mail and scan the code with
  your wallet. Your wallet then shows the pass at the door, and you can also sign in with it.

## Village tokens

Every pass brings 50 tokens per day. They have no money value: use them in the marketplace, to tip
a photographer, or pass them on to anyone. The link in your activation e-mail puts them into the
wallet. Once you have signed in to the wallet, it keeps your tokens on every device you use.

## Your identity

Your account page shows your village name (for example `osv-0042@<domain>`) and your Nostr key.
Belfry keeps the key safe for you. If you have your own Nostr signer, you can move your identity
to your own key on the security page; after that, the apps sign in with your own signer.

If you already use Nostr, you can also join with your key from the start: on the invite page, tick
**Join with my own Nostr key**. Your browser's signer extension shares your public key and signs
once to show the key is yours; Belfry never sees your private key. In the apps, sign in with your
own signer (a browser extension or a bunker link) instead of "Sign in with Open Source Village".

## Inviting friends

The village grows through people who know each other: you join with an invite, or by
[applying](/apply) and getting one from the stewards. With an
active pass you can invite a few friends: open [your pass](/me), then **Create an invite link**
and send or share it. Each link works once and expires after two weeks; the page shows which
links were used, and an unused one can be revoked to get the invite back.

## Village tokens

Every pass brings village tokens (50 per pass day). They are a game: they have no money value,
cannot be bought or exchanged for money, and expire when the event's services are turned off. We
do not replace tokens that are lost, sent to the wrong person or spent on an offer that was not
delivered.

# Community rules

Open Source Village is a place to make things together. These rules apply to everything you post

## Not allowed

- **Harassment and hate**: insults, threats, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and content
  that attacks people for who they are.
- **Other people's privacy**: posting someone's private information, or photos of people who did
  not agree to be photographed or shared. Ask before you take a picture of someone.
- **Illegal content**, and offers of anything illegal.
- **Scams and fraud**: fake offers, taking tokens without delivering, pretending to be someone
  else.
- **Spam**: repeated, unrelated or automated posts, and advertising that is not part of the
  village.
- **Selling for money in the marketplace** without the organisers' agreement: the marketplace is
  for village tokens and for giving. [TO FILL: confirm or change.]
- **Explicit or violent content**.

## What stewards do

Every report goes to all stewards. A steward looks at it and does one of these:

- **Hide** the content: it disappears from the directory, the marketplace and photos for everyone.
- **Dismiss** the report, when nothing breaks the rules.
- **Revoke the pass** of the person responsible, for serious or repeated breaks of the rules.
  Their account stops working, their pass is no longer valid at the door, they can no longer post,
  and what they posted disappears from the directory, the marketplace and photos.

## Your own content

You can delete your own offers and photos in the apps at any time. Leaving the directory is a tick
on "Your entry".
